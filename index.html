Teenagers spend too much time on phones, social media and games. Our card game helps them put the phone down and talk face-to-face. The cards ask the questions, so the talk does not feel like a lecture.

What We Offer

  • Card decks with a lesson plan for schools and school counselors
  • Team packs with a workshop guide for company wellness programs
  • Card sets for therapists to use with teens and families

How It Works

  1. Everyone puts their phones in one place.
  2. Take turns to pick a card and answer or do the task.
  3. Talk about the answers together. No right or wrong.

Example Cards

Question: What app do you open first in the morning, and why?
Challenge: No phone during dinner today. Who can do it?
Talk: What did you do for fun before you had a phone?
